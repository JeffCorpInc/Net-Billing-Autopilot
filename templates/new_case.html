Step 5 • NEPRA SRO 251(I)/2026 Schedule-V Undertaking

Applicant Declaration & Document Authenticity Consent

Mandatory Before Submission

Please review the declaration to the bottom (or click the arrow button) to unlock final submission.

I, the undersigned Applicant / Authorized Representative, hereby solemnly affirm, declare, and undertake as follows in connection with this Distributed Generation (Net-Billing) Application submitted to K-Electric under the NEPRA (Prosumer) Regulations, 2026:

1 Truthfulness & Accuracy of Entered Information

All personal, billing, and technical particulars entered in this application form—including my NADRA CNIC Number, Expiry Date, K-Electric 13-digit Consumer Reference Number, Sanctioned Load (kW), Connected Load (kW), Supply Phase, Proposed Solar PV Capacity (kW), and Inverter Rating (kW)—are true, complete, and accurate to the best of my knowledge and belief.

2 Authenticity & Legitimacy of Submitted Documents

All supporting documents declared and uploaded with this application—including the NADRA CNIC copy, latest K-Electric Electricity Bill, Registered Sale Deed / Allotment Letter / Lease Agreement, Landlord's No-Objection Certificate (NOC, where applicable), Single-Line Diagram (SLD), Schedule-V Affidavit, Vendor Checklist, and Inverter Datasheet / Protection Settings Letter—are authentic, genuine, unaltered, and legally valid copies of the originals.

3 Regulatory & Grid Safety Undertaking (NEPRA Reg. 3, 4 & 9)

I understand and agree that my proposed solar system capacity shall not exceed my sanctioned load, my connection must be a compliant 3-phase supply, and my grid-tied inverter incorporates certified anti-islanding protection (IEC 62116 / IEEE 1547). I shall not energize or parallel the distributed generation facility with the K-Electric distribution network prior to formal inspection and bi-directional meter commissioning.

4 Liability for Misrepresentation or Forged Documents

I acknowledge that if any information provided herein is found to be false, misleading, or inaccurate, or if any attached document is found to be forged, expired, or unauthentic during verification, K-Electric and the installer reserve the right to summarily reject this application or revoke net-billing interconnection approval without liability.

5 Consent to Automated Compliance & Staff Review

I hereby consent to the automated verification of my application pack (including PII-redacted cross-document consistency checks) and subsequent engineering review for K-Electric net-billing filing.

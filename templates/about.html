Product Mission

DATA722 Generative AI & Strategic Forecasting · Solar Onboarding Assistant

In early 2026, NEPRA replaced net metering with net billing under the NEPRA (Prosumer) Regulations 2026 (SRO 251(I)/2026). Under Regulation 3(5), K-Electric verifies application completeness within 5 working days, and applicants have only 3 working days to rectify missing items. Incomplete filings delay customer solar grid exports by weeks.

NetBill Assist is an internal intelligence tool for installer onboarding staff. It enforces deterministic regulatory validation, flags inconsistencies across identity and billing documents, generates bilingual customer rectifications (English & Urdu), and outputs draft Schedule-II applications.

The Human Gate (Zero Autonomous Action)

The system never submits applications, signs documents, or processes fee payments.

1
Staff Review

Staff inspects automated check verdicts and validates document pack.

2
Customer Signs

Customer reviews pre-filled draft PDF in person and physically signs.

3
Manual Filing

Authorized staff uploads signed dossier to K-Electric portal manually.

Code Decides, LLM Explains

Formats, numbers, dates, load calculations, and required checklists are decided 100% deterministically by Python code. LLM output never overrides hard-rule verdicts.

Privacy & Data Redaction

Zero CNIC numbers or billing reference IDs are ever transmitted to any LLM. Strict redaction runs prior to every model invocation.

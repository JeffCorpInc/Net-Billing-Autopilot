Net-Billing Regulatory Architecture

Under Regulation 3(5) of the 2026 NEPRA regime and the K-Electric Net Billing Guidelines (13 April 2026), the distribution company verifies filing completeness within 5 working days. If any document is defective or mismatched, the applicant is granted only 3 working days to rectify. Incomplete applications get rejected immediately, delaying rooftop solar export interconnection by weeks.

Rules as of: 2026-04-13 • DISCO: K-Electric (Karachi) • Academic: DATA722 Mid-Term Project

Deterministic Regulatory Rule Catalog (R1–R7)

Each rule implements an exact clause from the K-Electric Guidelines or NEPRA SRO 251(I)/2026.

R1 · CNIC Format Clause 2.1

Validates standard 13-digit Pakistani National Identity Card format: ^\d{5}-\d{7}-\d{1}$.

R2 · CNIC Expiry Clause 2.1

CNIC validity date must be strictly on or after filing evaluation reference date (2026-04-13). Expired cards rejected.

R3 · Capacity Cap Reg 3(1)

Proposed inverter output capacity must not exceed 100% of sanctioned load: proposed_kw ≤ sanctioned_load_kw.

R4 · 3-Phase Requirement Clause 3.2

Under K-Electric net billing guidelines, any generation exceeding 5 kW strictly requires a three-phase meter connection.

R5 · Connected Load Clause 4.1

Actual connected premise load must be strictly less than or equal to bill sanctioned load to prevent distribution transformer overloads.

R6 · Load-Flow Study Reg 3(7)

Interconnection capacity exceeding 250 kW triggers a mandatory distribution network load-flow stability study.

R7 · Required Documents Completeness Clause 2.2

Enforces mandatory dossier items: CNIC copy, latest paid electricity bill, title deed/proof of ownership, single-line diagram, Schedule-V affidavit, vendor checklist, inverter datasheet, and inverter settings letter. For tenants: owner's NOC. For commercial: NTN & Form-29.

Two-Layer Privacy & Credential Redaction Architecture

Strict defense-in-depth preventing accidental PII leakage to third-party model APIs.

Layer 1: Direct Field Exclusion

Before building the LLM prompt payload, builder functions omit cnic_number and account_number entirely from the Python dictionaries.

Layer 2: Regex Payload Scrubber

Serialized JSON strings are scanned via compiled regex patterns. Any 13-digit Pakistani CNIC or 13/14-digit K-Electric account reference is scrubbed to [REDACTED_CNIC] or [REDACTED_REF].

Unit-tested across all 21 synthetic cases in tests/test_redact.py: zero unredacted CNICs or bill account numbers ever reach the LLM wire.

Karachi Urban Semantics & Address Realities

Why LLM fuzzy matching is strictly required alongside deterministic code.

In Karachi municipal records and K-Electric consumer billing, identical addresses and names are routinely transliterated and abbreviated differently:

  • Name Honorifics & Transliterations: "Muhammad" vs "Mohd" vs "M.", "Syed", "Begum", "Chaudhry", "Khan".
  • Tenant vs Landlord: Lawful tenants apply with owner's NOC; bill name matches title deed rather than tenant CNIC.
  • Karachi Address Toponyms: "DHA Phase 6" vs "Defence Phase VI", "Clifton Block 2" vs "Block II Clifton", "Nazimabad 3" vs "Nazimabad No. 3".

Deterministic string matching fails on 90% of real Karachi packs. NetBill Assist deploys a specialized prompt paired with RapidFuzz fallback scoring. When confidence is borderline, the system safely verdicts UNSURE to route to human staff without failing the customer.
